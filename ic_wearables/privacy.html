IC Wearables

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

What We Collect

IC Wearables stores account details, style preferences, wardrobe selections, Discover activity, cart/order history, seller records, and AI-credit activity tied to your signed-in email.

Photos And AI Features

Face photos and uploaded images are used for colour analysis, outfit generation, and styling recommendations after consent. These inputs may be sent to configured AI providers only to provide those app features.

Payments And Retail Links

Payments are handled through Stripe when checkout is enabled. Affiliate or retailer links may open outside IC Wearables, where the retailer or payment provider applies its own policy.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account from the app profile screen. Public deletion instructions are available at Delete Account.

Contact

For privacy support, use the support contact listed on the Google Play listing and include the email for the account you want reviewed.
