Delete Your Account

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Delete In The App

Open IC Wearables, sign in, go to Profile, and press Delete account. Confirm the deletion request when prompted.

What Is Deleted

Deletion removes the signed-in login, synced profile, Discover posts, AI-credit ledger, app order records, wardrobe preferences, and app-owned data tied to your email.

What May Remain Elsewhere

Retailers, affiliate networks, payment providers, and app stores may keep purchase, payment, or compliance records under their own policies when you leave IC Wearables to complete a purchase or payment.

If You Cannot Sign In

Use the support contact listed on the Google Play listing and include the account email you want deleted.

See the Privacy Policy for more information.
